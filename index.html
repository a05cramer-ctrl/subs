ABOUT THIS CHANNEL

How it works

Four panels, four rules. Everything after the launch runs on its own.

01 · PICK

Pick any Twitch streamer

Search by name. Any channel with subs works, big or small. Anyone can launch.

02 · LAUNCH

Your dev buy goes to their subs

One transaction creates the coin on pump.fun and sends every coin of your first buy to the subs' escrow. You keep none.

03 · SIGN

Subs log in with Twitch

A sub logs in, Twitch confirms they're subscribed to that channel, they sign with a wallet. One Twitch account, one wallet, per coin.

04 · GRADUATE

Split at graduation, fees to the streamer

When the coin graduates, the dev buy splits equally between every sub who signed. The streamer claims the creator fees any time.

CLAIM

Subs and streamers

Connect the wallet you signed with to see every coin you're in and claim your share after graduation.

I'm the streamer

Fans launched a coin for you? Log in with your own Twitch account. Every coin's creator fees are yours, claimable any time, as often as they come in.

VERIFY IT YOURSELF

Every rule is on-chain

Each launch is one transaction: create, buy, send the whole buy to the escrow, tagged sub:<twitch channel id>. Each coin's pump.fun creator is its own fee wallet, so the fees can only go to that channel's owner.

FAQ

Questions from chat

Who gets the coins?

Everyone subscribed to the channel who logs in with Twitch and signs before the coin graduates. At graduation the whole dev buy splits equally between them.

Do gift subs count?

Yes. If Twitch says you're subscribed to the channel right now, Tier 1, 2, 3 or gifted, you can sign.

What does the streamer get?

All the creator fees. Every coin's creator on pump.fun is its own SUBS fee wallet, and only the channel's own Twitch account can claim from it, any time.

What does the launcher get?

Nothing from the dev buy: every coin of it goes to the subs in the same transaction. You launch it because you want the coin to exist, then buy like everyone else.

What if nobody signs?

The escrow burns the whole dev buy on-chain when the coin graduates.

What does SUBS see from my Twitch?

Only your Twitch id, your name and whether you're subscribed to that channel. We store a hash of your id so one account signs once.

Is this made by Twitch or the streamer?

No. SUBS is independent. Anyone can launch a coin for any channel; the streamer doesn't have to do anything to get their fees except log in.

When does signing close?

The second the coin graduates on pump.fun. The count is fixed at that moment, so the split can't move after.
